# Anonymous Questionnaire — System Usability Scale (SUS)

The "anonymous questionnaire" referenced in `docs/ethics_approval.md` (study
step 4) and `docs/consent_form.md`. It is the standard 10-item System
Usability Scale (Brooke, 1996), with "the system" substituted by "HapticWay"
— the accepted wording variant.

## Administration

- Administered **after** the post-session interview, as the final study step.
- Read aloud by the researcher and/or provided in large print, matching the
  accessibility commitment in the consent form. The researcher records the
  spoken response for participants who prefer not to mark a sheet.
- The sheet carries **only the anonymous participant code** (e.g. "P3") and
  the date — never a name (see `docs/DPIA.md`).
- Response scale for every item: **1 = Strongly disagree … 5 = Strongly
  agree**. Ask participants to give their first instinct rather than
  deliberate; if they cannot decide, mark the centre point (3).

## Scoring (researcher only)

1. Odd-numbered items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): contribution = **score − 1**.
2. Even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): contribution = **5 − score**.
3. Sum the ten contributions and multiply by **2.5** → SUS score, 0–100.

Interpretation: 68 is the commonly cited average across published studies;
above ~80 is generally regarded as excellent. Report per-participant scores
and the mean across the (minimum 5) participants; with n this small, report
the individual scores rather than relying on the mean alone.
