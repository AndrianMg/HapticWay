## What the study involves

1. A short interview about how you currently navigate (~10 minutes)
2. A 20-minute navigation task using the HapticWay app on the researcher's
   phone. Some trials use simulated obstacles controlled by the researcher.
   If you have usable vision you may be asked to wear a blindfold — this is
   **optional**.
3. A follow-up interview (~15 minutes) and a short anonymous questionnaire

A researcher stays within arm's reach of you at all times during the
navigation task. The route is indoors and checked in advance.

## Your data

- The app processes camera images **on the phone only, in real time — no
  image is ever saved** and nothing is sent to the internet.
- Interviews are audio-recorded and transcribed; the recording is deleted
  after transcription and your name is replaced with a code (e.g. "P3").
- We record your **level of sight loss** because this study evaluates an
  assistive technology. This is health information, so we ask for your
  explicit consent below. It is stored only as an anonymous variable.
- Anonymised results appear in a university project report. Nothing published
  can identify you.
- Full details: Data Protection Impact Assessment (`docs/DPIA.md`), available
  on request.

## Your rights

- Taking part is **voluntary**.
- You may **stop at any time, without giving a reason** — just say "stop".
- You may **withdraw your consent at any time**, before, during, or after the
  session, by contacting the researcher. Your data will be deleted.
- Withdrawing has no consequences of any kind.
