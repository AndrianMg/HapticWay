## Scope of approved study

Evaluation of HapticWay, an offline smartphone navigation aid for blind and
visually impaired users, comprising:

1. Pre-session interview (~10 min)
2. A 20-minute navigation task using the app, including Wizard-of-Oz
   blindfold trials run from the hidden researcher panel
3. Post-session interview (~15 min)
4. Anonymous questionnaire

## Recruitment criteria

Minimum 5 participants. Recruitment aims for a **mix**, not exclusion, across:

- Range of sight-loss severity (recorded as an anonymous demographic variable)
- Age
- Technology experience
- Cultural background

No criterion above is used to exclude a willing participant; the mix is sought
to surface diverse needs (see `docs/personas.md`).

## Participant safety

- Navigation tasks take place on a pre-walked, scripted indoor route free of
  uneven surfaces, stairs (unless scripted with a spotter), and traffic.
- A researcher remains within arm's reach of the participant at all times
  during blindfold trials.
- The participant may stop the session at any moment, for any reason, without
  explanation. The session also stops immediately on any sign of distress or
  any near-miss event.
